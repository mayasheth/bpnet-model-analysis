## Closed, do not reopen without new evidence

- **The converter as a model input.** Worse than plain ATAC in all four directions of the
  2x2. Two independent magnitude fixes both null, so the magnitude distortion diagnosed in
  F-013 was not the cause. No third transform.
- **Quantile mapping the painted track** (`0.37`). Matched the marginal exactly and made
  dynamic range worse, 18x to 9x against real DNase's 39x, costing 0.12 of shape.
- **A filter-width sweep for the shape control.** Superseded: the informative axis turned out
  to be DNase depth, not filter width.

  r 0.878, and the part of DNase counts carrying the benefit is by construction the part that
  differs from ATAC counts.

## What didn't work, and the bugs

- **Two attempts at the painted track's magnitude, both null.** Quantile mapping can only fix
  a marginal, not an ordering; forcing the marginal while the ordering is wrong (rank
  correlation 0.827) hands reference mass to over-ranked quiet bases and takes it from strong
  elements. A power transform fitted out-of-cell raised dynamic range 18x to 24x at a shape
  cost of 0.006, and moved the downstream delta from -0.0099 to -0.0105.
- **`build_reports.py:plain_punctuation` corrupted a published formula.** It protected fenced
  code but not inline code, so its paired-dash rule rewrote report 2's definition of
  `residual_pearson` as `r(observed (atac_pred, model_pred) atac_pred)`. Fixed to stash inline
  spans; all four reports rebuilt.
- **`pyBigWig.stats(type="sum")` needs `exact=True`.** Without it, it answers from zoom-level
  summaries: it returned 79 reads as the maximum over all of chr8, where the true value is
  12,458.
- **`pyBigWig.intervals(chrom)` OOMs on a dense track.** The painted track is dense because a
  softmax never emits zero, so chr1 is ~250M tuples. Stream in windows (`4.22`).
- **macs2 is not in the pixi env.** The only installation on Oak is inside ABC's built
  snakemake conda env, and putting its `bin` on PATH is not enough: it runs under the wrong
  interpreter and dies on `import MACS2`. Invoke as `$ENV/bin/python $ENV/bin/macs2`.
- **A `--array=0-23` silently dropped a chromosome.** `GRCh38.main.chrom.sizes` has 25
  contigs. Over-sizing an array is safe because the range guard exits cleanly; under-sizing is
  not.
- **I gated a wait on `ls -t log/*.txt | head -1`** and matched a previous run's log, which
  already contained the success string. Gate on the specific job's log, or better on the
  consumed files (`4.19`).
- **I deleted two TODO files with nine dangling references still pointing at them**, because I
  substituted a narrow grep for a broad scan that had timed out and treated the narrow result
  as the answer.

## Key decisions

- **Stop the converter as an input** (decision 2026-09-14). Its measured strength is shape,
  and shape is not even the operative property in every cell type. Machinery kept.
- **Elements for the converter are ATAC-derived** (D-19), so it never trains on regions chosen
  by the signal it predicts. The GM12878 converter could not match this (no ATAC-derived
  GM12878 set exists), so the two converters differ in element derivation.
- **Score the converter's profile with the raw estimator, never `profile_pearson`** (D-20).
  The latter is Gaussian-smoothed and is not on the same scale as the 1 bp ceiling: 0.774
  against a raw 0.799 and a ceiling of 0.831.
- **Every accessibility benchmark arm goes through one counting path** (D-21). Measured and it
  mattered: real ATAC as a bigwig scores 0.4572 against the July tagAlign arm's 0.4573, but
  real DNase as a bigwig scores 0.5280 against the July DHS arm's 0.5763.
- **Fit the magnitude transform out-of-cell.** gamma chosen in GM12878, applied to K562, so no
  target-cell DNase enters the choice. That is the deployment situation and it costs accuracy:
  K562 lands at 24x of its 39x target rather than the 108% GM12878 achieved.
- **One TODO list.** `todo/TODOLIST.md`. The other two were folded in and deleted; the
  convention is recorded in `CLAUDE.md`, which is auto-loaded every session.

## Gotchas

- **`profile_pearson` is 0.063-0.064 across all four accessibility inputs.** The H3K27ac
  profile head learns nothing whatever it is fed. Its target's 1 bp ceiling is 0.21.
- The painted converter track compresses dynamic range. Painted/observed ratio by
  observed-DNase quintile: 2.73, 0.88, 0.73, 0.71, 0.73. Thresholding at "less than one read"
  matched real DNase's zero fraction (74.5% against 71.3%) for 0.002 of shape.
- Tiled genome-wide prediction cannot reproduce a per-element count metric. Windows sit at
  fixed offsets and never coincide with an element centre, so `2.15`'s 0.835 becomes 0.687 as
  a painted track. Inherent, not a bug.
- **chrM has no held-out fold**, so no leakage-free model exists for it. It holds 9 of ~153k
  candidate elements and 17 negatives, none of which can reach a validation metric.
- THP-1: one DNase replicate, so no DNase shape ceiling and no use as a converter target. No
  ATAC, so it cannot train a converter. H3K27ac has two paired-end replicates of the same run
  type, so the ceiling that the panel is read against IS computable: 0.974 raw, 0.993
  corrected.
